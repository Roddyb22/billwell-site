Support

Need help with Billwell? Email support@mybillwell.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

You can also ask questions inside the app: open Ask Billwell from Home or Settings. Answers are generated on your device from the in-app help articles (and Apple Intelligence when your device supports it). Nothing you type is sent to us.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my logo and payment details?

Go to Settings › Business profile & branding for your name, logo, address and numbering. Go to Settings › Getting paid to add a payment link, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle, turn on Square, or connect Stripe.

How do I get paid in person with Square?

Install Square Point of Sale and sign in with your own Square account. In Billwell, open Settings › Getting paid and turn on Square Point of Sale. Open an invoice and tap Charge on card reader. New to Square? The same settings screen has a link to sign up; Square offers a free swipe reader to new sellers. You can also take tap and chip with Tap to Pay on iPhone in the Square app, or a Square contactless reader. You do not need a physical reader to try the handoff if Square is installed and signed in.

Does Billwell take a cut of my payments?

No. Clients pay you directly in your Square, Stripe, PayPal or other account. Billwell never handles the money. Those providers’ own fees apply.

How do I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Open the estimate and tap Convert to invoice. Items, tax, discount and photos are copied to a new invoice with the next invoice number.

How do I mark an invoice as paid?

Open the invoice and tap Record payment, or swipe right on it in the Invoices list. If you charged through Square or a Stripe payment link, Billwell can mark it paid automatically when that provider reports success.

What does the free version include?

The core app is free for up to 3 documents (invoices and estimates combined). Billwell Pro for your trade removes the limit and unlocks that trade’s tools. Some trades are a subscription; others are a one-time purchase. The paywall shows the price, billing period and renewal terms from the App Store before you buy.

How do I restore a purchase or manage a subscription?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on the Billwell Pro screen or in Settings. To change or cancel a subscription, use Manage Subscription in the paywall, or go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

On your device only — there is no Billwell account or Billwell cloud. Your data can be included in your regular device backups (iCloud or a computer) if you have them turned on. Use Settings › Backup & Restore to save a Billwell backup file to a folder you choose, such as your own iCloud Drive.

How does tech mode work?

Add a technician with a 4–6 digit PIN in Settings. Start tech mode from Settings. Techs see their jobs and time, not your prices. You return to the owner view with Face ID or your device passcode.

Can I change the invoice number format?

Yes. Settings › Business profile › Numbering lets you change the prefix and next number for invoices and estimates.
