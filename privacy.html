Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Billwell (“the app”) is published by Roderick Bourey (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. The short version: we don’t collect any of it, we don’t have a Billwell account system, and we don’t operate a Billwell server that stores your data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or data-collection SDKs. We cannot see your invoices, estimates, clients, business details, photos, payment links, keys, or technician information. Because we never collect or hold an account, there is nothing for us to delete on a server if you stop using the app — just delete the app (and any backup files you saved) on your devices.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — your business profile and logo, clients, saved items, invoices, estimates, shop records, inspections, and attached photos or short videos — is stored only on your device using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData). It stays there until you delete it or delete the app.

If you have enabled device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), this data may be included in your device backup, which is controlled by you and Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

If you use Backup & Restore, Billwell writes a backup file of your data to a folder you choose (for example your own iCloud Drive) or to a file you export. Those files are stored by you and your chosen storage provider; we never receive a copy. Your Stripe key and technician PINs are never included in backup files.

Camera, photos and microphone

You can attach photos to invoices and inspections, take inspection photos or short videos, and scan a VIN with the camera. The app uses the system camera and the system photo picker. When you pick from your library, the app only receives the items you choose and does not have access to the rest of your library. Microphone access is used only when you record an inspection video, so you can explain what you found. Media stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Face ID

If you use tech mode, Billwell can use Face ID (or your device passcode) so only you can leave tech mode and see prices, invoices and payments. Biometric data is handled by the device, not by us, and is never stored in Billwell.

Notifications

If you turn on service reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you. Those notifications are not sent through us, and we cannot see them.

Sharing and email

When you share a PDF or CSV, or email an invoice or estimate, the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet or Mail composer. The file is sent by the app or service you choose (for example Mail, Messages, or Files), under that service’s privacy terms. The app never sends your documents anywhere on its own.

Purchases

Optional Billwell Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple tells the app only which Pro products are active on your Apple Account so it can unlock features. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Payments between you and your clients

Billwell does not process, receive, or hold payments, and it never has access to your clients’ card or bank details. You can optionally connect payment services you already use. Each one works directly between your device and that provider; nothing passes through us.

  • Payment links and handles (for example a Stripe, Square, Clover or PayPal link, PayPal.Me, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle) are stored on your device and printed on your invoices.
  • Square Point of Sale: when you charge a card in person, the app opens your own Square Point of Sale app with the invoice amount, invoice number and client name filled in. Square takes the payment into your Square account and tells the app whether it succeeded, so the invoice can be marked paid. That information is handled by Square under its own privacy policy. New Square sellers can also open Square’s signup page from the app to request a free card reader; that page is provided by Square, not by us.
  • Stripe: if you paste a Stripe restricted API key, it is stored only in your device’s Keychain (not synced or backed up by Billwell). The app uses it to contact Stripe directly to create a payment link for an invoice (sending the amount, currency, invoice number and your business name) and to check whether that link has been paid. Your use of Stripe is governed by Stripe’s privacy policy. You can delete the key at any time in Settings › Getting paid.

Your clients pay you directly through the provider or method you choose. These tools are for payments for your real-world goods and services; they do not unlock Billwell features.

VIN lookup

If you decode a vehicle identification number (VIN), the app first checks the VIN on your device. To fill in year, make, model and related details it may send only that VIN to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration’s public vPIC API. That request goes from your device to NHTSA, not to us. We do not receive the VIN or the result.

On-device help and business setup (Apple Intelligence)

If your device supports Apple Intelligence, you can describe your business so the app can suggest fields, line items and wording, and you can ask questions in Ask Billwell. This runs entirely on your device using Apple’s on-device model. Your description and questions are not sent to us or to any Billwell server. On other devices, you pick an industry template and Ask Billwell uses the in-app help articles stored in the app. You always review suggestions before they are applied.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
